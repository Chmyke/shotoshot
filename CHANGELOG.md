# Changelog

What changed in each version of Shotoshot, newest first.

## 0.3.11 · 2026-10-23

- Second take: when the toolbar has to sit above your selection, it now steps aside to the left of the size badge instead of climbing over it.

## 0.3.10 · 2026-10-23

- Selecting near the bottom of the screen no longer parks the toolbar on top of the size and full-screen buttons. Everyone gets their own spot now.

## 0.3.9 · 2026-10-22

- The crosshair now picks black or white to match what's underneath, so it no longer plays hide-and-seek on pale pages.
- The capture click now always plays, so its switch left the menu. Prefer silence? An empty custom sound file does the trick (see the README).

## 0.3.8 · 2026-10-22

- **New** · Shotoshot now speaks 15 languages. Pick yours when installing, or switch anytime from the menu.
- **New** · Searching an image on Google now opens the results in your language, not just in English.
- No more squinting at the toolbar: the tool in your hand lights up blue with a little bounce, and your drawing colour sits in a white ring.

## 0.3.7 · 2026-10-17

- **New** · The bucket now stops at the lines you've already drawn, like paint poured into a colouring book. Right-click takes a fill back off.
- **Fixed** · Search an image on Google and your capture now waits behind the browser instead of vanishing.

## 0.3.6 · 2026-10-17

- Scroll over a selected shape to grow or shrink it. It keeps its colour and starts from its own size.

## 0.3.5 · 2026-10-17

- **New** · You can start a shape, or erase, outside the capture area. Arrows pointing at things off the edge, finally.
- The eraser rubs out freehand ink wherever it passes, and the mouse wheel sets its size.
- Hover anything and Shotoshot shows what a click would grab, before you click.
- Redrawn toolbar icons, now in the colour you're drawing with.

## 0.3.4 · 2026-10-17

- **Fixed** · Three screens under Wayland? Shotoshot now captures each one properly instead of squashing them into one. Thanks to our first bug report from the outside world.

## 0.3.3 · 2026-10-17

- The arrow tool is in your hand from the start, and text gets a real blinking cursor.

## 0.3.2 · 2026-10-15

- Installing takes one click: no questions, and Shotoshot starts by itself.

## 0.3.1 · 2026-10-15

- **Fixed** · "Search with Google Lens" works again in Brave and Edge, where it used to show an empty "expired" page.

## 0.3.0 · 2026-10-11

- **New** · The Board: write notes straight onto your desktop wallpaper, one board per screen. They stay under your windows until you clear them (Alt+B).
- Zoom and pan each screen of the Board on its own.
- A new eraser takes whole shapes in one click or one sweep.

## 0.2.0 · 2026-10-10

- **New** · Save a capture as an editable .shoto image. Reopen it later and every arrow, box and text can still be moved. Explorer even shows a thumbnail.
- **New** · Paint bucket: fill a shape, recolour a line, or flood the whole capture.
- Presentation mode shows your keyboard and mouse separately, with a choice of layouts.
- The capture appears instantly, Print Screen stays yours even when the Snipping Tool tries to take it back, and Alt+Tab no longer loses your annotations.
- Save as PNG or JPEG, and Page Up / Page Down move a shape above or below the others.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-09-24

- Capture a zone, annotate it with arrows, boxes, text and highlighter, or draw live on your desktop while you present.
