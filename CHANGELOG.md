# Changelog

What changed in each version of Shotoshot, newest first.

## 0.3.21 · 2026-10-28

- The faint frame that showed around the on-screen keyboard while you typed is gone: now it's just the keys you press, floating on their own.

## 0.3.20 · 2026-10-28

- New Text box tool (B): click and type to get text on a rounded colour panel, or drag first to choose how wide it is and let the lines wrap by themselves. The text sits in the middle and turns black or white so it always reads.
- A text box behaves like any other annotation: move it, recolour it, copy it, undo it. Dragging a corner of a wrapped box changes its width, not its font, and it reopens just as editable from a .shoto file.

## 0.3.19 · 2026-10-28

- The on-screen keyboard and mouse now keep out of sight: only the keys and buttons you press show up, right where they sit, then fade away. Your audience sees what you do, not a keyboard parked on the screen.
- To place them, pick Move keyboard and mouse in the menu: they show in full until you lock them again, and once locked they never catch a click meant for the window below.
- The keyboard layout menu is gone: a keyboard that only shows the keys you press takes no room, so it's always the full one now.
- Moving the spotlight no longer flashes a thin see-through gap at the edge of its shadow.

## 0.3.18 · 2026-10-28

- The spotlight's shadow now fades in gently around the light instead of starting at a hard edge, for circles and bands alike. Less flashlight, more stage lighting.
- The Pointer effects window gets a switch to show or hide the halo, and tweaking any effect turns the halo on by itself: no more adjusting settings for a pointer you can't see.

## 0.3.17 · 2026-10-28

- The pointer's ribbon trail now flows in one smooth curve, even when you whip the mouse around: no more little corners giving away how it was drawn.
- Shaking the mouse no longer sets off a giant circle around the pointer. It kept firing during ordinary back-and-forth moves, so it's gone for good.

## 0.3.16 · 2026-10-28

- New Pointer effects window, right under the halo in the menu: pick a disc, a ring or a neon glow, add a trail of sparks or stars, ripples and sounds on clicks, a spotlight that dims everything else, and shake the mouse to find it. The window shows each change live, before your audience sees it.

## 0.3.15 · 2026-10-24

- Saved captures are now simply named 001, 002, 003 and so on. Less clutter, same order, and the count picks up where the old names left off.

## 0.3.14 · 2026-10-23

- Hovering with the paint bucket now traces a dotted outline around what it's about to fill, instead of a pale tint that looked like see-through paint.

## 0.3.13 · 2026-10-23

- Taking several captures in a row? The next one opens on the area you just used, ready to annotate. Click outside it for a fresh one; after five quiet minutes it starts blank again.
- Ctrl+C right after typing a text now copies the image, as you meant, instead of quietly copying that text.

## 0.3.12 · 2026-10-23

Nothing you would notice in this version: the work is under the hood.

## 0.3.11 · 2026-10-23

- Second take: when the toolbar has to sit above your selection, it now steps aside to the left of the size badge instead of climbing over it.

## 0.3.10 · 2026-10-23

- Selecting near the bottom of the screen no longer parks the toolbar on top of the size and full-screen buttons. Everyone gets their own spot now.

## 0.3.9 · 2026-10-22

- The crosshair now picks black or white to match what's underneath, so it no longer plays hide-and-seek on pale pages.
- The capture click now always plays, so its switch left the menu. Prefer silence? An empty custom sound file does the trick (see the README).

## 0.3.8 · 2026-10-22

- **New** · Shotoshot now speaks 15 languages. Pick yours when installing, or switch anytime from the menu.
- **New** · Searching an image on Google now opens the results in your language, not just in English.
- No more squinting at the toolbar: the tool in your hand lights up blue with a little bounce, and your drawing colour sits in a white ring.

## 0.3.7 · 2026-10-17

- **New** · The bucket now stops at the lines you've already drawn, like paint poured into a colouring book. Right-click takes a fill back off.
- **Fixed** · Search an image on Google and your capture now waits behind the browser instead of vanishing.

## 0.3.6 · 2026-10-17

- Scroll over a selected shape to grow or shrink it. It keeps its colour and starts from its own size.

## 0.3.5 · 2026-10-17

- **New** · You can start a shape, or erase, outside the capture area. Arrows pointing at things off the edge, finally.
- The eraser rubs out freehand ink wherever it passes, and the mouse wheel sets its size.
- Hover anything and Shotoshot shows what a click would grab, before you click.
- Redrawn toolbar icons, now in the colour you're drawing with.

## 0.3.4 · 2026-10-17

- **Fixed** · Three screens under Wayland? Shotoshot now captures each one properly instead of squashing them into one. Thanks to our first bug report from the outside world.

## 0.3.3 · 2026-10-17

- The arrow tool is in your hand from the start, and text gets a real blinking cursor.

## 0.3.2 · 2026-10-15

- Installing takes one click: no questions, and Shotoshot starts by itself.

## 0.3.1 · 2026-10-15

- **Fixed** · "Search with Google Lens" works again in Brave and Edge, where it used to show an empty "expired" page.

## 0.3.0 · 2026-10-11

- **New** · The Board: write notes straight onto your desktop wallpaper, one board per screen. They stay under your windows until you clear them (Alt+B).
- Zoom and pan each screen of the Board on its own.
- A new eraser takes whole shapes in one click or one sweep.

## 0.2.0 · 2026-10-10

- **New** · Save a capture as an editable .shoto image. Reopen it later and every arrow, box and text can still be moved. Explorer even shows a thumbnail.
- **New** · Paint bucket: fill a shape, recolour a line, or flood the whole capture.
- Presentation mode shows your keyboard and mouse separately, with a choice of layouts.
- The capture appears instantly, Print Screen stays yours even when the Snipping Tool tries to take it back, and Alt+Tab no longer loses your annotations.
- Save as PNG or JPEG, and Page Up / Page Down move a shape above or below the others.

## 0.1.0 · 2026-09-24

- Capture a zone, annotate it with arrows, boxes, text and highlighter, or draw live on your desktop while you present.
