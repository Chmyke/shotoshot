# Contributing

Shotoshot is developed by a single maintainer.

- **Bug reports and ideas are welcome.** Open an issue and say what you did, what you expected
  and what happened. The Windows version, the number of screens and their scaling usually matter.
- **Code contributions are not accepted for now.** Pull requests are closed without review. The
  reason is practical: a single copyright holder keeps the licensing simple. This may change.
- **Security problems** are reported privately: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Development happens in a private working repository. This repository receives the complete source
of every published version, one commit per publication.

You are free to fork under the terms of the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). Please do not distribute modified
builds under the Shotoshot name or icon.
